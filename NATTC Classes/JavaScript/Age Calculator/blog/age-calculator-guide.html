The Complete Age Calculator Guide

AgeVault is more than a basic age calculator. This guide walks you through every feature — from a simple age lookup to zodiac signs, planet ages, birthday countdowns, and all the extra tools available.

Basic Age Calculation

To calculate your age, simply navigate to the Age Calculator, enter your date of birth, and click Calculate Now. AgeVault immediately shows your age in years, months, and days — along with total weeks, total days, total hours, minutes, and seconds.

The "Calculate Age On" Feature

By default, AgeVault uses today's date. But you can change the "Calculate Age On" field to any date — past or future. This is useful for: calculating someone's age on a specific historical date, finding out how old you'll be on a future date, or retroactively calculating an age for a document.

Zodiac Sign

Your Western zodiac sign is automatically shown based on your birth month and day. AgeVault also shows your Chinese Zodiac animal, based on your birth year's position in the 12-year cycle.

Birthstone

Each birth month has a traditional gemstone associated with it. January is Garnet, February is Amethyst, and so on through December's Turquoise. Your birthstone is shown automatically in the results.

Generation

AgeVault identifies which generation you belong to — from the Silent Generation to Generation Alpha — based on your birth year. This gives useful social and cultural context to your age.

Planet Ages

This is one of AgeVault's most unique features. Every planet orbits the Sun at a different speed, so your age in "planet years" varies. You'll see your age on Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune — a fun way to visualize the scale of the solar system.

Leap Year Info

AgeVault detects whether you were born in a leap year and counts exactly how many leap years you've lived through. Leap year births (February 29) are highlighted as a special badge.

Birthday Countdown

The Birthday Countdown tool gives you a live, real-time countdown — ticking down days, hours, minutes, and seconds to your next birthday.

Other Tools

  • Age Difference Calculator — compare ages between two people
  • Retirement Calculator — countdown to your chosen retirement age
  • Life Timeline — visual milestones from birth to 100
  • Pregnancy Calculator — due date and current week
  • Work Experience Calculator — total professional tenure

Share & Copy Your Results

Once you've calculated your age, use the Share, Copy, and Print buttons to save or share your results. The Share button uses the native Web Share API on supported devices, making it easy to send your age breakdown to friends or family.
