FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my exact age calculated?

We use precise calendar arithmetic — subtracting birth date from today accounting for actual month lengths and leap years. The result is exact down to the second.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your date of birth never leaves your device. We don't use servers for calculations, and we don't collect any personal data.

Can I calculate age for any date — past or future?

Yes. Use the "Age On" field to choose any date. Calculate someone's age on a specific historical date, or find out how old you'll be on a future date.

How does the heartbeat counter work?

We use 70 beats per minute as the average resting heart rate and multiply by the number of minutes you've been alive. It's an estimate — real heart rates vary with activity, age and health.
