FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AgeVault and how age calculation works.

🧮 Calculator

How is my exact age calculated?

We calculate the difference between your birth date and the selected date using precise calendar arithmetic — accounting for leap years, varying month lengths, and timezone-safe date handling. The result includes years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

Can I calculate age for a past or future date?

Absolutely. Use the "Calculate Age On" field to select any date — past or future — and AgeVault will compute the age as of that specific date. This is useful for historical research, planning, or curiosity.

Why do the months and days sometimes seem off by one?

This is due to the different lengths of months. For example, February has 28 or 29 days. When calculating "days remaining" within a partial month, we always use the actual number of days in that specific month, which is the most accurate method.

What is the most accurate way to calculate age?

The most accurate method is to count calendar years completed, then remaining calendar months, then remaining days — which is exactly what AgeVault does. For total days or hours, we use Unix timestamp difference in milliseconds.

🔒 Privacy & Data

Is my date of birth stored or shared?

No. All calculations run 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your date of birth never leaves your device. We do not use servers to process calculations, and we do not collect or transmit any personal data.

Do you use cookies?

We use only essential cookies — specifically localStorage to remember your dark/light mode preference. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that identify you personally.

♈ Zodiac & Astrology

How are Western zodiac signs determined?

Western zodiac signs are based on the position of the Sun at the time of birth, divided into 12 roughly equal segments of the ecliptic. Each sign corresponds to a date range — for example, Aries covers March 21 to April 19.

How is the Chinese Zodiac determined?

The Chinese Zodiac follows a 12-year cycle, with each year represented by an animal. The cycle runs: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig. Your sign is determined by your birth year.

What is a birthstone?

A birthstone is a gemstone traditionally associated with each calendar month. For example, January's birthstone is Garnet, July's is Ruby, and December's is Turquoise. These associations vary slightly by tradition; AgeVault uses the modern Western list.

📅 Leap Years

What is a leap year?

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365 — adding February 29th. A year is a leap year if: it is divisible by 4, AND (not divisible by 100, OR divisible by 400). So 2000 was a leap year, but 1900 was not.

What happens if I was born on February 29?

If you were born on February 29 (a leap day), AgeVault detects this and highlights it. In non-leap years, your birthday is typically celebrated on either February 28 or March 1 depending on convention. The calculator uses the actual birth date as entered.

🪐 Planet Ages

How are planet ages calculated?

Each planet orbits the Sun at a different speed. Your age in "planet years" = your age in Earth years ÷ that planet's orbital period in Earth years. For example, Mars has an orbital period of ~1.88 Earth years, so a 30-year-old Earth person is about 15.96 Mars years old.
