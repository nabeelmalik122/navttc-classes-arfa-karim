Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

TL;DR: AgeVault performs all calculations entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your date of birth or any personal data to our servers. The only storage we use is localStorage for your dark/light mode preference.

1. Information We Collect

Date of Birth Inputs: When you use AgeVault's calculators, any dates you enter are processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. These values are never transmitted to any server, database, or third party. They are not stored in cookies or localStorage.

Theme Preference: We use localStorage to remember whether you prefer dark or light mode. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Contact Form: If you choose to contact us via the contact form, you voluntarily provide your name, email address, and message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

2. Cookies

AgeVault does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any cookies that identify you personally. The only browser storage we use is localStorage for your theme preference (a key named av-theme).

3. Third-Party Services

AgeVault loads resources from the following third-party CDNs:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter and Syne typefaces. Google may log your IP address per its standard logging policies.
  • Cloudflare CDN — for Font Awesome icon delivery.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, advertising networks, or any other tracking or analytics service.

4. Children's Privacy

AgeVault does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our tools are informational and require no account creation or personal data submission to function.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data held by AgeVault to request access to, correct, or delete. If you submitted a contact form message and wish to have it deleted, please email us at hello@agevault.app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of AgeVault after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at hello@agevault.app or via our Contact page.
