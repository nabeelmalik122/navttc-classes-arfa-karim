About Us

Measuring Time, Beautifully

AgeVault was built with one goal — to make time feel meaningful, elegant, and surprisingly fun to explore.

Our Mission

Most age calculators are functional but forgettable — plain inputs, bare outputs, no personality. We believe a tool you use every day deserves to be beautiful. AgeVault's mission is to make time-related calculations feel like a premium experience, not an afterthought.

We obsess over every pixel, every transition, every interaction — so that when you open AgeVault, you immediately feel the difference.

What Makes AgeVault Different

There are hundreds of age calculators on the internet. Most were built in the late 2000s and haven't been touched since. AgeVault was built from scratch in 2026 with a modern design system, accessibility-first approach, and a genuine respect for user privacy.

  • Zero data collection: Everything runs in your browser. Your date of birth never leaves your device.
  • Beyond basic age: Zodiac signs, planet ages, birthstones, generation data, leap year counts — all in one place.
  • Dark mode native: Designed in light and dark from day one, not bolted on as an afterthought.
  • Accessibility first: Full keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, screen reader support, and WCAG-compliant color contrasts throughout.
  • Performance obsessed: No heavy frameworks, no bloat. Pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript that loads instantly.
